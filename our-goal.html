Our Goal

About us an What we do

Established in 2008, our journey began with a simple yet profound mission: to bring more exposure to Islam and spread its light through the Karoo region. We are deeply passionate about our cause, driven by the belief that through our actions, we can make a meaningful difference in the world.

At our core, we are committed to giving back to our community. Through various outreach programs, sponsorships, and dedicated efforts to build places of worship where Islam is scarce, we strive to embody the values of compassion and generosity taught by our faith.

Our team consists of six dedicated individuals who have devoted themselves wholeheartedly to our mission. Inspired by the Quran, the words, “A man's true wealth hereafter is the good that he does in this world to his fellows,” we endeavour to honour these principles in all that we do.

Thank you for joining us on this journey. Together, let us continue to illuminate the world with the light of Islam and make a positive impact on the lives of others.
